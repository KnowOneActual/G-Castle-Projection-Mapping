# 03 - Sample Assets Guide

This guide lists reliable sources for sourcing media to use on the G-Castle.

## 🧱 Structural Transformation Loops
These are used to make the castle appear as if it is changing shape or material.

*   **Destruction/Breaking:** 
    *   [Vecteezy Cracking Wall](https://www.vecteezy.com/video/69832739-cracking-and-breaking-brick-wall-destruction-effect-4k-shot)
    *   [Pixabay Brick Wall Assets](https://pixabay.com/videos/search/brick%20wall/)
*   **Textures:** Use these to turn the house into "Gold," "Ice," or "Wood."

## 👻 Holiday Themes

### Halloween
*   **Mixkit Spooky Collection:** [Free Halloween Videos](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/halloween/)
*   **Dancing Skeletons/Ghosts:** Search YouTube for "Green Screen Halloween Loops."

### Christmas
*   **Snowfall/Freezing:** Look for "Snow Overlay" on Pixabay or Pexels.
*   **Gingerbread House:** High-res texture images can be used as static backgrounds in Resolve.

## 🛍️ Premium Recommendations
*   **AtmosFX:** [Official Store](https://atmosfx.com/) - Best for character-driven stories.
*   **Digital Pressworks:** [Video Packs](https://digitalpressworks.com/house-projection-mapping-videos/) - Specifically formatted for house mapping.

## 🛠️ Usage Notes
1.  **Format:** Always prefer `.mp4` or `.mov` with H.264 or ProRes encoding.
2.  **Resolution:** Aim for 1080p. 4K is nice but can be slow to render in DaVinci Resolve on older hardware.
3.  **Alpha Channels:** If a video has a black background, use the "Screen" composite mode in Resolve to make the black transparent.
