# 01 - Projector Hardware Guide

Choosing the right hardware is critical for high-impact house projection mapping. For the G-Castle, we need a balance of brightness (lumens), throw ratio (distance), and weatherproofing.

## 🌟 Key Specifications for House Mapping

| Feature | Recommended | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brightness** | 3000 - 5000 ANSI Lumens | Houses are large and ambient light from streetlights can wash out the image. |
| **Resolution** | 1080p (Full HD) | Higher is better (4K), but 1080p is the sweet spot for budget and file size. |
| **Throw Ratio** | Short Throw (0.5 - 0.8) | Allows the projector to be closer to the house, reducing shadows and tripping hazards. |
| **Contrast** | High (10,000:1+) | Important for black levels and making the animations "pop." |

## 📽️ Recommended Projectors (2025/2026)

### 1. The Pro-Budget Pick: Epson Home Cinema 1080
*   **Lumens:** 3400
*   **Pros:** Reliable color, widely available, easy to find used/refurbished.
*   **Cons:** Not short throw; needs distance.

### 2. The Short-Throw King: BenQ LH860ST (or similar series)
*   **Lumens:** 4000 - 5000
*   **Pros:** Laser light source (long life), short throw lens, incredible brightness.
*   **Cons:** Higher price point.

### 3. The Used Market Strategy (Epson PowerLite Series)
*   **Model:** PowerLite 5xxx series (e.g., 530, 520).
*   **Pros:** Can often be found on eBay for under $300. Designed for classrooms, they are bright and durable.

## 🌦️ Weatherproofing & Protection

### The "Hush Box" (DIY Solution)
Since most projectors are not outdoor-rated, a protective box is required:
1.  **Enclosure:** Use a heavy-duty plastic tote or a custom wood box.
2.  **Airflow:** Install two computer fans (Intake/Exhaust) to prevent overheating.
3.  **Lens Port:** Use high-clarity glass (anti-reflective is best) for the projection window.
4.  **Elevation:** Keep the box off the ground (on a stand or table) to avoid ground moisture and snow.

### Standalone Playback (The "Brain")
*   **Micca MPLAY-HD:** Reliable, hardware-based loop, no OS crashes.
*   **Raspberry Pi (DMX Control):** If you want to sync with Christmas lights, use a Pi running Falcon Player (FPP).
