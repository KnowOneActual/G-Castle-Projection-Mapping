# 02 - Project Plan: G-Castle Projection Mapping

This plan outlines the steps required to go from a blank house to a fully mapped, repeatable holiday show.

## 🏁 Phase 1: Setup & Mapping
1.  **Site Inspection:** Determine the primary viewing angle (where the audience will stand).
2.  **Projector Placement:** Mark the exact spot (permanent markers in the yard are recommended).
3.  **Outline Creation:** 
    *   Connect a laptop to the projector.
    *   Use the **Digital Pressworks Mapper** to trace every architectural detail (windows, doors, turrets).
    *   **Crucial Step:** Save this outline as a high-resolution image file (the "Mask").

## 🎨 Phase 2: Content Creation
1.  **Mask Setup in Resolve:**
    *   Import your mask image into DaVinci Resolve.
    *   Use the mask as a guide layer (lower opacity) to place your videos and animations.
2.  **Scene Design:** 
    *   *Halloween:* Skeletons in windows, dripping slime on walls, "crumbling" effects.
    *   *Christmas:* Snow falling, house turning into gingerbread, wrapping paper transitions.
3.  **Rendering:** Export the final video in a format compatible with your Micca player (usually H.264/MP4).

## 🛠️ Phase 3: Hardware Deployment
1.  **Enclosure Assembly:** Build or prep the "Hush Box" for outdoor use.
2.  **Signal Testing:** Ensure the Micca player loops correctly and the video aligns with the house.
3.  **Weatherproofing:** Check seals and airflow fans.

## 🚀 Phase 4: Showtime & Repeatability
1.  **Event Timing:** Set up a timer or smart plug to turn the projector on at dusk.
2.  **Repeatability Checklist:**
    *   Place projector on markers.
    *   Power on Micca.
    *   Confirm lens focus.
